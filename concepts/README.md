# Carmen Wilde — homepage design directions

## Current direction: same vibe, more inventive layouts

A fixed **Next design** button cycles through the original homepage, Exhibition, and Field Notes on desktop and mobile. It appears only on these three review homepages, not on artwork, catalog, or contact pages. The older, out-of-brief experiments below are intentionally excluded.

- **Exhibition (`gallery-window.html`)** has a desktop gallery sidebar, enormous typographic title, and a single uninterrupted panoramic painting, followed by exhibition-style captions and a quiet two-work display. Best for a serene fine-art gallery feel.
- **Field Notes (`field-notes.html`)** has a centered journal masthead, layered artwork collage, handwritten-feeling captions, a running line of type, and staggered painting sequence. Best for a personal, imaginative artist's presence.

These differ in navigation, hero composition, scale, pacing and art presentation—not merely colour or spacing. Both keep the approved paper/pine palette and Carmen's own paintings.

The two older colour-theme experiments below preceded this brief and are **not the recommended direction**.

These are **review-only homepage concepts**, not separate stores or complete site migrations. The current preview and the live Wix site are unchanged. Links to artwork, commissions and contact lead to the existing staging site's corresponding pages; shopping still bridges to Wix and must not be treated as launch-complete. All concept pages are `noindex,nofollow`.

## Nocturne (`nocturne.html`)
- **Stance:** immersive, dark-gallery presentation; the paintings become luminous against charcoal.
- **Layout:** split hero and staggered exhibition grid, restrained brass accent, high-contrast serif typography.
- **Strong at:** dramatic art presentation and a premium gallery feeling.
- **Trade-off:** darker and more formal than Carmen's current bright personality.

## Studio Notes (`studio-notes.html`)
- **Stance:** colourful artist's journal; a playful, welcoming path through the work.
- **Layout:** cobalt and terracotta typography, paper background, offset artwork panels and handwritten-style note.
- **Strong at:** personality, warmth and a memorable artistic identity.
- **Trade-off:** more expressive type and colour may compete with some paintings.

Neither direction replaces the existing site until Carmen selects one and the checkout, inventory and contact launch gates in `../README.md` are resolved.
