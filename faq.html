Questions & answers.

A few practical notes about original artwork, commissions and ordering.

What does original fine art cost?

As a guide, Carmen’s published rates are typically $1.00 per square inch for smaller pieces and $1.50 per square inch for larger works. Examples from the current FAQ: 8 × 10 inches — $80; 12 × 12 — $144; 16 × 20 — $480; 16 × 40 — $960; 24 × 48 — $1,728; 48 × 36 — $2,592; 48 × 60 — $4,320. Confirm the final quote directly with Carmen.

Can I commission a painting?

Yes. Carmen creates custom acrylic works based on a meaningful photograph or place. Learn about commissions or send an inquiry.

How do commissions work?

Commissioned paintings start at $1.50 per square inch. Carmen paints an original interpretation of your photo, not a photo-finish reproduction. In-person or virtual consultations start at $125 per hour; remote clients can email or mail a photograph and consult virtually. Please confirm current rates and details directly with Carmen.

Do you offer art rentals?

Yes. Rentals are available for staging, sales centres and businesses; rates and selections are discussed individually. See art rentals.

How does shipping work?

Domestic shipping within Canada is offered for a flat fee. Local Edmonton-area delivery may also be available for a flat fee. Read the shipping policy.
