Privacy & safety

This static website does not store contact-form entries or payment information. The contact form opens your own email application, and messages you send are handled by your and Carmen’s email providers. GitHub Pages hosts this website and Google Fonts supplies its typography; those providers may receive standard technical requests when you visit. For purchases, the existing Wix store handles checkout and payment information. Read the current store’s terms.

Payment methods

Credit and debit cards are supported through the current online store. E-transfers and offline payments may be arranged directly with Carmen.

Online checkout currently takes place on the existing store. This GitHub-hosted website does not collect payment details.
