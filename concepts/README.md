# Carmen Wilde — homepage design directions

These are **review-only homepage concepts**, not separate stores or complete site migrations. The current preview and the live Wix site are unchanged. Links to artwork, commissions and contact lead to the existing staging site's corresponding pages; shopping still bridges to Wix and must not be treated as launch-complete. Both concept pages are `noindex,nofollow`.

## Nocturne (`nocturne.html`)
- **Stance:** immersive, dark-gallery presentation; the paintings become luminous against charcoal.
- **Layout:** split hero and staggered exhibition grid, restrained brass accent, high-contrast serif typography.
- **Strong at:** dramatic art presentation and a premium gallery feeling.
- **Trade-off:** darker and more formal than Carmen's current bright personality.

## Studio Notes (`studio-notes.html`)
- **Stance:** colourful artist's journal; a playful, welcoming path through the work.
- **Layout:** cobalt and terracotta typography, paper background, offset artwork panels and handwritten-style note.
- **Strong at:** personality, warmth and a memorable artistic identity.
- **Trade-off:** more expressive type and colour may compete with some paintings.

Neither direction replaces the existing site until Carmen selects one and the checkout, inventory and contact launch gates in `../README.md` are resolved.
